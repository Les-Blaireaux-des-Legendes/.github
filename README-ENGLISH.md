# 🔎🦡 Who are we?

</div>

Founded in 2024, **The Legendary Badgers** began as a collective bringing together enthusiasts and professionals from the fields of cybersecurity, digital investigation and open-source intelligence (**OSINT**).

As its activities expanded, the collective formally became a [French non-profit association under the 1901 Law](https://annuaire-entreprises.data.gouv.fr/entreprise/les-blaireaux-des-legendes-993380013) the following year.

This evolution reflects our commitment to structuring our practices over the long term, strengthening our collective capabilities and carrying out our activities within a rigorous, ethical and public-interest-oriented framework.

As part of this approach, the association may also contribute to humanitarian or assistance-related initiatives, including countering misinformation, reporting abusive online content and behavior, combating child sexual abuse material, and supporting victims of cyberharassment.

Our objective remains the same: **to put digital investigation techniques and open-source intelligence at the service of concrete, responsible and useful actions.**

Our name is also a deliberate nod to the French intelligence world and reflects our desire to make these disciplines more accessible to non-specialists, without ever compromising their rigor.

**Taking our work seriously, without ever taking ourselves too seriously.**

👉 **Badger sometimes, Legend always!**

---

## 🎯 Our missions

→ Participate in digital investigation exercises and **Capture The Flag (CTF)** competitions, as well as missing-person search initiatives in collaboration with specialized organizations.

→ Develop and maintain a high level of expertise in **OSINT**, cyber-origin intelligence, data analysis and advanced digital investigation techniques.

→ Maintain continuous technological and methodological monitoring in order to keep pace with evolving tools, practices and threats within the digital environment.

→ Contribute to the development, promotion and wider adoption of open-source intelligence while advocating an **ethical, responsible and collaborative** approach to OSINT investigations.

→ Encourage knowledge sharing and professional development, particularly through educational, immersive and gamified approaches.

→ Contribute to the visibility and international reach of French-speaking communities active in digital investigation, OSINT and cybersecurity.

→ Help prevent and combat misinformation, online abuse and illegal content, while paying particular attention to supporting victims of cyberharassment.

## 🐾 Why the badger?

The badger embodies three qualities that are central to the way we work:

→ **Resilience** — returning to the task, again and again.

→ **Method** — structuring information in order to understand it.

→ **Adaptability** — operating effectively in complex and changing environments.

A discreet, persistent and methodical animal, the badger relies less on brute force than on observation, patience and its ability to find alternative paths.

**Sharp instincts. Structured thinking. A sharpened keyboard.**

> **In the forest as in cyberspace, the surface informs, the depth reveals.**

# 🌐 The Burrow 🌐

</div>

**Recruitment is currently closed and is not intended to remain permanently open.**

We deliberately favor a qualitative approach over continuous growth in membership.

Our organization is built around team stability, complementary profiles, knowledge transfer and the maintenance of a high level of operational standards.

Internal cohesion, communication and individual responsibility play a central role in the way we operate. Our structure is deliberately collaborative and minimally hierarchical, with the aim of facilitating information sharing and collective effectiveness.

**The association also encourages partnerships with organizations and individuals who share its values and areas of interest.**

These collaborations may take the form of resource sharing, joint projects, tool provision or testing, exchanges of expertise, logistical support or participation in common initiatives.

# 🧩 Challenge design 🧩

</div>

**Taking part in CTFs is not enough: designing them is also part of what we do.**

We believe that learning is most effective through experience.

Our challenges are therefore designed as genuine immersive investigations combining digital investigation, scenario design, critical analysis and problem-solving.

Our approach focuses on creating realistic environments inspired by our professional expertise and contemporary issues such as **misinformation, influence, information operations, open-source intelligence and cyber threats.**

The objective is to place participants in situations where they must search, analyze, cross-reference and prioritize information in order to extract meaning from it.

---

## <img width="52" src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/da/Minist%C3%A8re_des_Arm%C3%A9es_et_des_Anciens_Combattants.png/330px-Minist%C3%A8re_des_Arm%C3%A9es_et_des_Anciens_Combattants.png"> — Égide [2025]

We had the honor of co-designing a scenario for the **Égide** edition of the Capture The Flag organized by the [French Ministry of Armed Forces](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ministere-des-armees_%C3%A9gide2025-activity-7287453919184650240-HmIA/), in partnership with the French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs and [VIGINUM](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/VIGINUM).

This challenge focused on **countering information manipulation** and was designed to reflect realistic operating conditions, with the aim of strengthening participants’ resilience against influence campaigns and contemporary information operations.

Through an interactive scenario, simulated media environments and varied informational content — articles, social-media posts, videos and contextual messages — participants were confronted with realistic situations involving the circulation and manipulation of information.

They had to analyze sources, identify influence mechanisms, compare competing narratives and develop a deeper understanding of information-manipulation strategies.

The objective was to encourage lasting critical-thinking reflexes and strengthen participants’ ability to distinguish reliable information from deliberately constructed narratives.

🔗 **Event link:** [Capture The Flag Égide 2025](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/actualites/capture-the-flag-fait-son-retour-2025)

## <img width="60" src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/fr/thumb/a/ad/Logo_Bpifrance.svg/langfr-500px-Logo_Bpifrance.svg.png"> — Mission Vérité [2025]

We also designed the **Mission Vérité** CTF for the [Bpifrance.io](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7369632940462350336/) community.

The challenge took place in two phases:

* a first stage conducted entirely online;
* a second hybrid phase held in person at the Accor Arena during **BIG**.

Participants progressed through an investigation combining digital research, puzzle-solving and strategic analysis.

Each challenge revealed a **flag** — an identifier, data point or key clue — required to move forward within the scenario.

Beyond the competitive dimension, the objective was twofold: to immerse participants in a coherent narrative and to allow them to apply digital investigation techniques that can be directly transferred to real-world contexts.

🔗 **Event link:** [Capture The Flag Mission Vérité 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250915014744/https://ctf-bpifrance.io/rules)
