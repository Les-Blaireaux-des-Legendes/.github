# 🧬 01 — Organizational Profile

**Founded:** 2024  
**Legal status:** French non-profit association (Law 1901)  
**Domain:** Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) · Cyber Investigation · Digital Forensics  

The Legendary Badgers began as an informal collective of cybersecurity and intelligence practitioners and evolved into a structured association to formalize operations and reinforce methodological rigor.

Beyond competitive OSINT, the group contributes to **public-interest digital investigations**, including:
- Countering online misinformation
- Supporting victims of cyberharassment
- Monitoring harmful or illicit online content (including child exploitation-related threats)
- Promoting safer digital environments

> The name is a cultural reference to a French espionage universe, reflecting a core intent:  
> **make intelligence work accessible without sacrificing rigor.**

# 🌐 03 — Operational Doctrine (“The Burrow”)

This ensures:
- Operational cohesion
- Methodological consistency
- High investigative standards

The collective prioritizes:
- Depth over scale
- Stability over expansion
- Expertise over volume

- Responsibility is distributed
- Communication is fluid
- Trust is operational currency
